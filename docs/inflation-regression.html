Return vs. inflation — where the statistical relationship shows up, and why it disappears

Simple linear regression: each stock's annual return (Dec-to-Dec), compared with that year's average inflation rate. The panels below show the relationship over the last 5 years (2021-2025, the only window where it comes out statistically significant), plus a chart showing why it disappears once more years are added.

OLS regression (scipy.stats.linregress). Each point = one stock-year observation; the line is the regression fit through those points. The conventional significance threshold is p < 0.05 (dashed line in the second chart). Once 6+ years are included, the relationship no longer clears that threshold — the 2021-2025 signal is dominated by the 2022 inflation shock, not a stable long-term pattern.
