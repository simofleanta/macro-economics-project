How much of the BVB stock gains are real — and how much is just inflation?

A stock's nominal price can climb a lot, but if prices across the economy climb just as fast, your purchasing power isn't really growing. The charts below compare nominal return (the price gain as it appears on the exchange) with real return (that same gain, adjusted for Romania's inflation) for 5 BVB-listed stocks, August 2021 – December 2025.
